Vibrant Campus Life

Student Clubs & Organizations

Education at Pacific International extends far beyond the classroom walls. We encourage students to explore their interests through a wide variety of student-led clubs and organizations. These groups provide valuable leadership opportunities and allow students to connect with peers who share their passions, whether in Debate Club, Robotics, Model UN, or the school newspaper.

Athletics

Our athletics program, the Pacific Wave, is built on the principles of sportsmanship, teamwork, and personal growth. We offer a wide range of competitive sports including basketball, soccer, swimming, and track and field. Our state-of-the-art facilities include a modern gymnasium, an Olympic-sized swimming pool, and extensive playing fields.

Arts & Culture

The arts are an integral and vibrant part of our community. Students can participate in our award-winning choir, orchestra, and drama productions, or hone their skills in our visual arts programs. Our campus features a 500-seat modern theater, dedicated art and ceramics studios, and music practice rooms to support our budding artists.
